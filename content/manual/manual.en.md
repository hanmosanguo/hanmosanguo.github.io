# Ten Thousand Foes: Yellow Turban Rebellion — Annals of Pacifying the Realm (Manual)

> **"The empire, long divided, must unite; long united, must divide."**
> In the last years of the Eastern Han, the court was rotten and calamity struck without end. Zhang Jiao of Julu styled himself the "General of Heaven" and, with his brothers Zhang Bao (General of Earth) and Zhang Liang (General of Man), preached the Way of Great Peace and handed out charmed water, crying **"The Azure Heaven is dead; the Yellow Heaven shall rise. In the year Jiazi, great fortune shall come to all under heaven!"** In an instant the common folk of every quarter bound their heads in yellow cloth and raised the banner of revolt. Sorcerers wove their spells, raising skeleton armies from the wild bones and driving the refugees mad, and the whole land was plunged into misery.
> Troubled times breed heroes. With the realm teetering on the brink, all hope rests on a peerless general who, three-foot blade in hand and his troops at his back, will fight from the village countryside all the way to Zhang Jiao's great city, sweep away the foul miasma, and restore the Han!

## I. Introduction: A Lone Army Breaks the Rebels, Step by Step

### 1. Troops Are Your Life — Never Retreat an Inch
In Ten Thousand Foes: Yellow Turban Rebellion, you, my lord, take command of a famed general of the Three Kingdoms and lead your **"Troops"** on the campaign against the Yellow Turban rebels.
- **Troops are your foundation**: A general's strength is never one man's valor. Your "Troops" are your lifeblood (HP). Once they are worn down to nothing, you are surrounded, defeated, and taken captive.
- **Eastward, stage by stage**: The army marches out from the "Village Countryside," passes through the "Yellow Turban Main Camp," "Zhang Liang's City," and "Zhang Bao's City," and at last strikes straight at "Zhang Jiao's City." The host advances in force and never turns back; you may maneuver freely across the fields in every direction.
- **A hundred battles and five Chieftains**: On the field you will meet foes beyond count: wild Skeletons, coerced Refugees and Turban Swordsmen at first, then elite blades and shields, crack archers and iron cavalry in red, silver and gold armor, chain-swinging brutes, heavy infantry behind great shields, Armored Soldiers with mountain-cleaving blades, and Sorcerers who mutter curses to mark you in secret. Every wave brings a new menace. Every few waves, the five Rebel Chieftains of the Yellow Turbans, **Deng Mao, Gao Sheng, Zhang Liang, Zhang Bao and Zhang Jiao**, take the field themselves. Only by felling a Chieftain can you shatter the rebels' nerve and open the road ahead! (If you still hunger for battle after Zhang Jiao falls, step into the endless **"Death Match Mode"** and face the dark otherworldly foe **"Black Warrior"**!)

### 2. Plan Your Moves, Win Gold and Glory
- **Auto-attack; positioning is king**: Your general automatically cuts down any foe who draws near, and the Martial Skills you have learned unleash themselves in turn as they come off cooldown. All you need do is mind your footwork: dodge the arrow circles lobbed by archers, the shrinking curse seals of Sorcerers, and the lethal warnings of the Chieftains.
- **Commands**: On keyboard, march with **WASD or the arrow keys** and pitch camp with the **Space bar**; **P** pauses (the game also pauses when you switch away from the window; press any key to resume); **Esc** returns to the main screen. On a phone, the **left half of the screen is a joystick** and a **tap on the right half** acts as the Space bar. Xbox and PS controllers are also supported: **left stick** to march, **A** to confirm and pitch camp, **B** to go back, **Start** to pause. Your first deployment begins with the **Tutorial**, which you can revisit at any time from the main menu.
- **Slay foes, seize gold, break through in the heat of battle**: Felled rebels drop copper coins, gold ingots, red coral, jade and gems. Picking up this wealth does more than fill the army's coffers as "Gold"; it also tempers your men as "experience." When experience is full, the army gains sudden insight on the field (**Level-Up card draw**) and you choose one of three (or four, if Fortune smiles) strategic boons: learn a new **Martial Skill**, open up the **Three-Army Camps**, or sharpen your existing skills and your own constitution.
- **Rations and chests from heaven**: Rebels often drop tasty army rations on the field: Steamed Buns, Drumsticks, Big Pork Cutlets, Lobster, even Roast Suckling Pig. Simply walk over them to reward your men and restore Troops (each ration restores a fixed share of your Max Troops; the finer the fare, the more it heals). Defeat a Gold Elite or a Yellow Turban Chieftain and a glittering **"Grand Chest"** may drop. Open it and gold and silver gush forth amid flying streamers, granting a great boon that lifts your own Martial Skills or Camps several levels at once!

### 3. Worship at the Ancient Temple, Rally Anew
Whenever you cut down a Yellow Turban Chieftain and raise your weapon to proclaim "Chieftain Vanquished," the mists of battle clear, and a solemn **Ancient Temple** (its hall enshrines the three golden statues of Shakyamuni, the Medicine Buddha and Maitreya) always rises ahead. Step through its gates and the battlefield freezes for a while, leaving you free to make ready at leisure:
1. **Lucky Draw before the Buddha** (based on Taiwanese moon-block divination: two crescent blocks are tossed — one up, one down means the gods say yes): Offer incense money at the altar (those who enter after slaying a Chieftain worship for free) and toss the rosewood blocks. "Blessed!" brings radiant Buddha-light and a blessing from heaven; "Laughed Off" makes the Buddha smile; "Denied" does no great harm to your campaign.
2. **Recruit Troops**: At the tent on the left, turn the gold you seized in battle into freshly recruited troops to replenish your losses, and set in advance how your Troops will be allotted among the three Camps when they deploy.
3. **Treasure Shop and Temple Repairs**: At the stall on the right, buy legendary Divine Weapons, famed steeds and secret military manuals. You may also donate incense money generously ("As You Wish" or "With All Sincerity") to restore the Ancient Temple from a "Humble Shrine" through "Rising Incense" and "Majestic Halls" to "Golden Splendor," building up **Blessing** so that the chests, treasures and gold dropped on later battlefields grow ever richer!
4. **Break Out**: Should your Troops be wiped out on the field and you be surrounded and brought to your knees, you may still choose, out of sheer loyalty and courage, to **"Break Out,"** falling back to the Ancient Temple to regroup your remnants and plan anew! (Those who enter the temple in defeat must pay incense money for the Lucky Draw, and cannot repair the temple.) When you enter "Death Match Mode" after slaying Zhang Jiao, **one last Ancient Temple** remains for you to prepare in; after that there are no more temples, not even for felling the Black Warrior. Defeat there is the end of the road, and you must start over from the beginning.

Once your preparations are done and you step out of the temple gates, the mountains, rivers, trees and rocks ahead have all changed, and a brand-new battlefield awaits.

## II. The Heroes and Their Peerless Martial Skills

### 1. 【Lord of the Beautiful Beard】 Guan Yu (courtesy name Yunchang)

> **"Nine feet tall, with a beard two feet long; a face like ripe jujube, lips as if rouged; phoenix eyes and sleeping-silkworm brows, a noble bearing and awe-inspiring presence."**
> A native of Jieliang in Hedong. When a local tyrant abused his power, Lord Guan slew him out of righteousness and fled to become a wanderer of the rivers and lakes. Later, in Zhuo Commandery, he met Liu Bei and Zhang Fei, and the three swore brotherhood in the Peach Garden, vowing to work with one heart, to serve the nation above and to bring peace to the people below. Lord Guan wields the **Green Dragon Crescent Blade** (also called the "Cold Beauty Saw"), weighing eighty-two jin. His bladework is bold and sweeping, each swing a fan-shaped horizontal cut, and amid ten thousand troops he takes an enemy general's head as easily as reaching into a pouch!

#### Guan Yunchang: Legends of His Peerless Martial Skills

* **【Dragon Soaring in the Sky】**
  Legend has it that in the early days of his campaign, as Lord Guan slept at an ancient ridge of the Taihang Mountains, he dreamed of an azure dragon high in the ninth heaven that, moved by his loyal heart, coiled down and bestowed on him the supreme intent of the blazing blade.
  When he unleashes it, Lord Guan raises the Green Dragon Crescent Blade overhead in one hand, pointing at the heavens. In an instant the winds and clouds change color, a colossal dragon head thrusts out of the clouds, and from its jaws a deluge of true fire pours upon the earth! The flames cascade down the blade and in a blink become a raging sea of fire that spreads forward with Lord Guan's every step. Every Yellow Turban bandit who enters the flames burns alive, and wails fill the field; the rebels at the edge, seeing heaven's wrath descend, lose their wits in terror and flee, casting off helmets and armor!

* **【Overreaching Dragon】**
  Once, outside the walls of Guangzong, Lord Guan was ringed by several thousand elite Yellow Turban troops, a forest of blades and shields, not a gap to be seen. In that crisis he recalled what he had read by night in the Book of Changes, the hexagram Qian: "The overreaching dragon will have cause to repent; fullness cannot last," and in a flash grasped the secret art of "turning the blade into a circle, endlessly renewed."
  Lord Guan grips the Green Dragon Crescent Blade in both hands across his chest and, with the force of a thousand-jin stance, spins out, man and blade together, like a raging whirlwind! The crescent blade becomes a full moon of chilling light, and he can even move as he spins; as his mastery deepens, the blade's aura grows ever longer and vaster. Any foe who dares to step into the circle is dazed by that unmatched, savage gale and hurled out of it on the spot, truly not a drop of water nor a needle could get through!

* **【Dragon in the Field】**
  In his early years of wandering, Lord Guan once beheld a wondrous sight in a great marsh: a hidden dragon rising from the deep to range freely over the fields. He wove that untamed, unbridled momentum into his swordplay.
  Whenever the enemy ranks pack tight, Lord Guan lets out a great shout and thrusts his blade forward; the blade flares with green light and several green dragon shadows tear out from its tip! As his cultivation deepens, the dragons grow larger and more numerous, and like unleashed divine dragons they race in every direction toward the edge of the field. Wherever they pass, the rebel lines are smashed to pieces, and the rebels flounder as if mired in mud.

* **【Might of the Central Plains】**
  Lord Guan's boundless righteous spirit pervades heaven and earth, and when he glares with knitted brows he carries a kingly dominance that awes without anger.
  When he unleashes it, Lord Guan sinks into a low stance, trails the crescent blade and stamps the earth; at once the ground quakes, the mountains shake and fissures burst open! Golden shockwaves of dominance, like a boulder dropped into a still lake, spread outward ring after ring from beneath his feet, and the battlefield around him erupts with several resonant shock circles. The Yellow Turban rebels caught within the golden waves and shock circles go weak in the knees under that mountain-shaking pressure and tremble violently, unable to advance or even raise a blade, left to stand like puppets and take Lord Guan's thunderous strokes!

* **【Flooding the Seven Armies】**
  Lord Guan knew his military classics well and deeply understood the cunning stratagem of "using water as a weapon" (a foretaste of the later Flooding of the Seven Armies at Xiangfan).
  When Lord Guan swings his blade and stirs the waterways of the four quarters, rolling channels first take shape across the battlefield from near to far, trapping the rebels in muddy, deep water so that they slow to a crawl and their assault collapses, just as the military maxim says: **"Strike an army when it is half across the river!"** Then ten-thousand-foot white waves roar down from the ends of the channels and sweep across the entire field! Ordinary rebels in their path are flushed off the field and drowned by the towering flood, and even a Yellow Turban Chieftain is battered by the heavy surf until his blood churns; all flames on the field are doused in an instant, finally dwindling to trickles of water that seep slowly into the yellow earth.

### 2. 【Zhao Zilong of Changshan】 Zhao Yun (courtesy name Zilong)

> **"Eight feet tall, with thick brows and large eyes, a broad face and a full jaw, a mighty and imposing presence."**
> A native of Zhending in Changshan. Born into a chaotic age and carrying benevolence and righteousness in his heart, he threw himself into the saddle and the sword to save the common people of the realm. Zilong wears a white robe and silver armor and wields a long spear of bright silver. His spearwork is swift as a startled swan and quick as lightning; even an ordinary strike is a chain of three rapid thrusts, high, middle and low, the spear-shadows overlapping like a fan! Stranger still, Zilong's sincere soul has struck a cross-world resonance with a mysterious war god from another realm in purple armor and a dragon helm, the **"Summoner Zilong"** (the Purple Dragon), and strange apparitions often lend their might in battle!

#### Zhao Zilong: Legends of His Peerless Martial Skills

* **【Spear of the Soul】** (innate skill; mastered without drawing a card)
  From boyhood Zilong studied under a master and deeply attained the highest state of "man and spear as one." This skill is his born instinct; it takes up no strategy slot and bursts forth of its own accord whenever his fighting spirit peaks (and the higher your Intelligence, my lord, the more often it triggers).
  Zilong whirls and thrusts amid the ranks, and his long spear at once splits into several white rainbows that shoot out in a ring in every direction! Each spinning silver spear, the instant it strikes its first rebel, pins him fast to its head and, with the force of ten thousand jin, bulldozes him all the way to the end of the field before it stops! The impaled rebel becomes a human battering ram, sending the comrades in his path sprawling in all directions. Truly, "one spear to the soul, and a hundred evils recoil!"

* **【Seven In, Seven Out】**
  In years past, Zilong rode alone through the chaos of battle to save his lord, treating a million troops as nothing (the very might of the Seven In, Seven Out at Changban).
  When he unleashes it, a fiery red protective aura surges around Zilong, and he vaults onto a snow-white steed to gallop across the field like a gale! His long spear sweeps in great arcs, the tip tracing streaks of white-blue light and layer upon layer of afterimages. Throughout, Zilong is Invincible, proof against blade and spear, and wherever the white horse's iron hooves and his savage spear-wind pass, the rebels all around are blasted away and badly hurt, truly reviving the unrivaled glory of "Blood stains his robes, red through the armor; who at Dangyang dares contest his edge?"

* **【Phoenix Cry, Five Cuts】**
  This is the ultimate secret of the "Hundred Birds Hail the Phoenix Spear" taught by Zilong's master!
  Zilong gives a light flick of the wrist and one thrust tears the sky; the spear tip gathers true fire and becomes a blazing phoenix with wings spread wide, whose long cry rings straight through to the end of the field! As his spearmanship advances, a single thrust can release up to five fire phoenixes at once, flying in a fan and growing larger as they go, beyond anyone's power to stop. The phoenixes are especially fearsome against heavily armored foes, with the power to burn gold and melt iron, and across the vast land they cross they leave trails of scorching fire that long refuse to die, so that the rebels following behind burn themselves by stepping into the flames!

* **【Burning Heart】**
  Zilong lived his whole life upright and aboveboard, his heart full of burning blood, and that sincerity tears through the void itself, calling forth his comrades from another world, the **"Purple Dragon Guards,"** to fight at his side!
  Each time he uses this skill, several purple-armored guards, proof against blade and spear, appear out of thin air beside Zilong. These hot-blooded fellows say not a word; they heft the great basins in their hands and hurl them with all their might at the nearest cluster of rebels. In an instant the basins pour out rolling channels and towering white waves that sweep the whole row of rebels off their footing and leave them struggling to move! Stranger still, now and then a careless guard splashes out a basin of old, blackened **"dirty water"** by mistake, and once that black-purple poison wave touches them, any foe who is not a Chieftain is poisoned and drops dead on the spot, the most dread-inspiring spectacle on the battlefield!

* **【Rising Dragon Strike】**
  When the enemy surges and the battle hangs in the balance, Zilong cries to heaven a secret phrase of the other world (*"Only six left upstairs!"*), and his whole body blazes with a ten-thousand-foot divine light, transforming on the spot into that mysterious **"Summoner"** in purple armor and dragon helm!
  While transformed, Zilong is indestructible. He sinks low to gather strength, then leaps from the ground with an earth-shaking **"Rising Dragon Fist"** that surges to the clouds and blasts the rebels around him into the air! At the same time, springs boil up and whirlpools burst forth across the ground, and several immense sky-propping pillars of water erupt skyward one after another, blasting the Yellow Turbans who stray into the water ring, armor and all, up beyond the ninth heaven to crash heavily back down, dousing every flame of war along the way!

* **【Fighting Dirty】**
  As the saying goes, "War is the way of deception." Faced with a locust-like horde of Yellow Turban rebels who both wield sorcery and wear heavy armor, even a man as benevolent and righteous as Zilong knows full well: "Why stand on ancient etiquette when dealing with villains?"
  Zilong throws back his head with a great laugh and suddenly whips out from under his silver armor and battle robe a gleaming black machine of the other world, the **Black Iron Gatling Gun**! Gripping it in both hands, he strides forward firing as he goes, muzzle flames roaring, ten thousand streams of metal bullets pouring out like a storm and automatically locking onto the nearest foes to tear them apart! Be it a thousand troops and ten thousand horses, iron armor and bronze shields, all crumble to dust in an instant like figures of clay before this era-defying, "Fighting Dirty" firepower!

## III. The Camps: Pitching Camp, Messenger Doves and the Art of Terrain

The ancients who excelled at war first made themselves unassailable. On the campaign against the Yellow Turbans, if you, my lord, grasp the art of the Camps on the field, you may command the three armies to **pitch camp**, forming a mutually supporting position with you and fighting shoulder to shoulder.

### 1. Each of the Three Camps Has Its Duty
1. **【Infantry Camp】**: A great camp of blade-and-shield infantry, firm as a fortress. When the gates swing open, elite swordsmen charge out in groups to meet and halt the approaching enemy. The Infantry Camp has the sturdiest defense and greatly blunts the rebels' assault, the most dependable bulwark at the front.
2. **【Cavalry Camp】**: A camp of swift, peerless light cavalry. When the horns sound, squadrons of iron cavalry gallop out of camp and charge straight through the enemy ranks with thunderous force, made to break dense formations. If you yourself are mounted on a Famed Steed on the field, the camp's cavalry are inspired by you and rally of their own accord to charge toward where you are!
3. **【Archer Camp】**: The camp of crack marksmen who can pierce a willow leaf at a hundred paces. Stationed in the line, they ceaselessly lob storms of arrows at the rebels within range. The archers never miss, and their arrows track the enemy's movements and strike true every time they fall!

### 2. Pitching and Recalling Camp: The Art of Allocating Troops
- **One key to pitch camp, split your forces to garrison**: On the field, simply press the camp command (Space bar) and the army builds a rampart at your feet in the order **"Infantry Camp → Cavalry Camp → Archer Camp"** (up to three may stand at once; if the ground underfoot is too crowded, a nearby clear spot is found automatically, but camps cannot be pitched on puddles, horse barricades or rock piles). When you pitch camp, a portion of your own Troops, in the proportions you set beforehand at the Ancient Temple, is allotted to the camp and becomes its defending strength (durability). A third of newly arriving rebels will also be drawn to the camps, sharing the pressure with you.
- **Breaking camp and rejoining (Recall Camp)**:
  - **Automatic recall on the march**: When you push the army forward and a rear camp drifts out of view (off the screen), or when you successfully slay that stage's Chieftain (Boss), the camp strikes itself and is recalled, and **all Troops still in the camp rejoin you unharmed, returning to your side**.
  - **Camp destroyed, troops lost**: Conversely, if a camp is besieged by rebels until its Troops reach zero, it collapses and vanishes outright, and every soldier garrisoned within dies for the cause and cannot be recovered. Knowing how to guard your camps and read the situation is therefore the great wisdom of a commander!

### 3. Messenger Doves and the Combined Strike of the Three Armies
Camps are not merely things to be held to the death. Once the troops within have gathered their strength, they unleash earth-shaking **Camp Skills**:
- **【Messenger Dove】**: Whenever a camp's skill comes off cooldown, the camp releases a **dove** wreathed in rosy glow (blue for the Infantry Camp, red for the Cavalry Camp, yellow for the Archer Camp), which beats its wings, calls out the skill's name, and flies straight toward your position! If you move to catch the dove, that camp immediately unleashes its ultimate; if you miss it in the heat of battle, the dove returns to camp upon reaching the edge of the field, rests briefly, and flies out again:
  - **Infantry Camp skill 【Steady Advance】**: A great host of elite swordsmen pours out of the camp from every side at once, advancing outward with earth-shaking war cries and cutting down all the rebels who press upon the ramparts, forcing them back!
  - **Archer Camp skill 【Rain of Arrows】**: A row of crack archers rises above the camp and looses ten thousand arrows at once! Countless fire arrows fall from the sky and turn the ring around you into a roaring sea of flame, so that rebels who step inside keep burning (fire is especially effective against armored foes)!
  - **Cavalry Camp skill 【Death Charge】**: The camp's iron cavalry see red and, depending on the camp's size, form a **"line," a "wedge" or even a "double wedge,"** eyes bloodshot as they surge forward in a frenzy, charging all the way to the edge of the field before rebounding back! Any rebel struck by the cavalry is flung backward along the line of the charge, spitting blood in flight until he dies; at the same time the charge inspires you and the allied camps, greatly raising the whole army's Attack Speed and fighting strength!
- **【Three-Army Strike (Three-Camp Combo)】**: When you hold all three camps — Infantry, Cavalry and Archer — on the field and every camp's special is charged and ready, no messenger dove is needed. The three armies act as one and unleash the earth-shaking **"Three-Army Strike"** on their own! In an instant, double the swordsmen push out on every side, a vast sea of fire rains from the sky, and two waves of blood-crazed cavalry thunder back and forth. The three armies roar as one and sweep a thousand armies aside!
- **【Three-Camp Iron Wall (Full Defense Mode)】**: When all three camps stand together, your own martial skills are all on cooldown, and your troops have fallen below half, leaving you on the brink, the three camps automatically trigger the **"Three-Camp Iron Wall"**! Every camp's Attack and Attack Speed surge and gain mighty knockback, and all blades and arrows of the three camps turn inward, concentrating their fire to guard you to the death!

### 4. Terrain Bonuses: Adapt to the Land, for War Is the Way of Deception
The *Art of War*, in its chapter on terrain, says: "Terrain is the soldier's ally." The battlefield holds boulders, forests, shrubs and open plains, and where you pitch your camps directly shapes the strength of your armies:
1. **【Boulder Cover】**: Pitch a camp beside a great boulder and you gain the **"Boulder Cover"** advantage, borrowing the rock's natural stronghold to raise the camp's defense. But the rock also blocks sight and passage, so the side hidden behind it cannot strike at the enemy.
2. **【Treeline Ambush】**: Pitch a camp beside woods or shrubs:
   - The **Infantry Camp** thrives under the cover of the trees. Its overall power rises, and it can spring the **"Stealth Ambush"** stratagem, surprising any rebels who come near the camp for extra heavy damage!
   - Conversely, the **Archer Camp** has its sight blocked by branches and leaves, and the **Cavalry Camp** finds its horses tangled in the undergrowth. Both lose strength when pitched at the forest edge.
3. **【Open Ground】**: Pitch a camp on wide, open ground with no cover on any side:
   - The **Cavalry Camp** can gallop unhindered, and the strength of its charges grows greatly!
   - Conversely, the **Infantry Camp** has no strongpoint to hold on open ground and is exposed on all sides, so its defense suffers slightly.

## IV. Wonders of the Realm: Treasures, Divine Weapons, Famed Steeds and War Manuals

On the battlefields of the Yellow Turban campaign, slaying a mighty foe or paying respects at an ancient temple may bring you a legendary treasure of the Three Kingdoms. Treasures that must be performed (stratagems, carried blades and spears, mounting a famed steed) are first stored in your **"Treasure Pouch"** and unleashed one by one in good order. Divine weapons and war manuals, meanwhile, become a permanent protective power for the rest of the run, wreathing you in radiant golden light.

Rare treasures come by fortune, not by seeking: **each treasure appears only once per run** (whether from battlefield drops, chests or the temple shop). When a famed general finds his own **signature weapon** (Guan Yu with the Green Dragon Crescent Blade, Zhao Yun with the Dragon-Gall Silver Spear), man and weapon become one, and **both its power and duration are multiplied by 1.5** — though such a find is all the rarer.

### 1. Brocade Pouch Stratagems (Stratagem Treasures: Moving Mountains with Intelligence and Might)
Pick up a Brocade Pouch on the battlefield, and you will carry out a stunning stratagem:
- **【Sweep a Thousand Armies】** (scales with your **Might**): With a roar of "Sweep a thousand armies!", you summon a colossal Green Dragon Crescent Blade that blots out the sky and sweeps the entire battlefield in a thunderous arc! Wherever its blade-light passes, every demon soldier and rebel — except enemy chieftains — is reduced to ashes! The higher your Might, the wider the realm the great blade sweeps.
- **【Golden Bell Armor】** (scales with your **Might**): A divine art of supreme yang strength! A radiant golden bell shield appears around you, making you impervious to blade and spear and immune to every attack for a time. Even a thousand besiegers cannot cost you a single soldier. The greater your Might, the longer the bell protects you.
- **【Chain of Iron】** (scales with your **Intelligence**): Pang Tong offered the chain stratagem, and now it is put to use on land! When triggered, three giant wooden stakes are driven in according to the camps' positions (outside the three camps if you have three; with you as the third point of a triangle if you have two), and glowing iron chains are drawn taut between them! While the chains last, rebels inside the barrier are forcibly pushed out, and the tide of rebels outside is held fast beyond the circle, unable to take one step past the line! (Note: with fewer than two camps on the field, the formation cannot be set.)
- **【Tiger Drives Wolf】** (scales with your **Intelligence**): A masterstroke of Xun Yu! The instant it is picked up, the battlefield holds its breath, and with a sharp cry the Yellow Turban grunts all around you lose their wits. They abandon you and turn their blades to attack the **"Elite Chief" or "Rebel Chieftain (Boss)"** in a frenzy!
- **【Two Tigers Fight for Food】** (scales with your **Intelligence**): Another stratagem of Xun Yu! The Yellow Turbans within range grow suspicious of one another and turn on their own, pairing off to fight and slaughter each other on the spot!
- **【Circling the Tree Thrice】** (scales with your **Intelligence**): Drawn from Cao Cao's *Short Song Ballad*: "The moon is bright, the stars are few; magpies fly south. They circle the tree three times — on which branch can they rest?" Once cast, every Yellow Turban in the circle loses its bearings, a green light spinning above its head, and can only turn dizzily in place, never again able to come within half a step of you or your camps!

### 2. Carried Blades and Spears (Divine Weapons: Hovering Guardians, Stronger with Every Battle)
Once picked up, these divine blades and spears take solid spirit form and hover at your side to fight with you. Each spirit weapon has its own "durability (duration)," and the higher your level, the longer it stays to aid you in battle!
- **【Qinggang Sword】**: Originally Cao Cao's treasured sword, carried by Xiahou En and later seized by Zhao Zilong at Changban Slope, it cuts through iron like mud! Once picked up, it becomes a blue-glowing sword that spins around you at high speed, and wherever its edge passes, it **completely ignores all heavy armor defense**, cutting down the red-, silver- and gold-armored elite troops!
- **【Heaven-Reliant Sword】**: Cao Cao's own blade, which together with the Qinggang Sword is counted as one of the two peerless swords. It becomes a pair of majestic golden-glowing swords standing guard at your left and right. They hold back at rest, but the moment a rebel dares enter their sensing range, both swords automatically lash out in swift slashes!
- **【Dragon-Slaying Saber】**: "Supreme in the martial world is the saber that slays the dragon" — a domineering heavy blade drifted into the Three Kingdoms from another realm. It becomes two blazing great sabers guarding your left and right; upon sensing the enemy, they not only chop heavily at nearby foes but also loose air-splitting **crescent blade qi**. Against a Yellow Turban Rebel Chieftain (Boss), the blades' might is even more overwhelming!
- **【Twin Swords】**: The pair of swords forged when Liu Bei raised his army, one gold and one silver, never apart. Once picked up, the two swords float on either side of you and weave up and down in a mysterious **"figure-8"** path, the pair working as one to shred enemies from every direction.
- **【Seven-Star Blade】**: The family heirloom of Minister Wang Yun, which Cao Cao once carried into the chancellor's residence to assassinate Dong Zhuo. Once picked up, the blade hangs at your side and ceaselessly fires cold-gleaming flying blades in **four directions** — front, back, left and right — piercing the rebels along their path.
- **【Ancient Ingot Saber】**: The famed blade of Sun Jian, the Tiger of Jiangdong. Once picked up, it becomes a huge bronze blade hanging before you; the blade swells, lifts high again and again with its hilt as the pivot, then accelerates down in a crushing, thunderous chop that sends whole swaths of the enemy sprawling!
- **【Dragon-Gall Silver Spear】**: The signature divine spear of Zhao Zilong of Changshan! It becomes a giant silver spear guarding your whole body, and the instant a rebel enters its range, it **thrusts in rapid succession** faster than the ear can catch thunder! Whether a hundred or a thousand close in at once, all are pierced by ten thousand spear-shadows in an instant and forcibly flung beyond the circle, not one escaping!
- **【Green Dragon Crescent Blade】**: The signature divine weapon of Guan Yunchang. Picking it up resonates with the blade's spirit; rather than conjuring a new blade, it wreathes you in a blue-green divine glow, **doubling your own attack power** for a time, every stroke weighing like Mount Tai!

### 3. Heirloom Divine Weapons (Treasures of the Realm: Protection for the Whole Run)
Once obtained, these weighty divine weapons stay in effect for the whole run, and their power rises with your level:
- **【Sky Piercer Halberd】**: The peerless divine weapon of Lü Bu, the Marquis of Wen! Blessed by this halberd, your basic attack grows sharper with each level, and on every third strike you unleash an extra, overwhelmingly domineering **full-moon sweep**!
- **【Feather Fan】**: The white-crane feather fan held by Zhuge Liang, the Sleeping Dragon. With a gentle wave of the fan, no stratagem fails; it keeps raising your **Intelligence** as you level up, and steadily shortens the cooldown of all your peerless martial skills.
- **【Zhuge Repeating Crossbow】**: The "Yuanrong" war engine that Chancellor Zhuge Liang devised by improving the repeating crossbow. With this divine crossbow's blueprints, both the **Attack Speed and Attack of your camps** keep growing stronger as you level up.
- **【Imperial Jade Seal】**: Carved by the First Emperor of Qin from the Heshi Jade, inscribed "By Heaven's mandate, long life and prosperity," it is the supreme treasure on which the Han's mandate rests. Whoever holds the Seal is favored by Heaven, and the **gold** won from slain foes flows ever more abundantly and multiplies without end as you level up. When it is obtained, chime bells ring out together and you cry, "By Heaven's mandate, long life and prosperity!"
- **【Dilu】**: The divine steed that saved Imperial Uncle Liu by leaping Tan Creek in a single bound of thirty feet, now become an heirloom talisman. When your troops are all but wiped out, heaven and earth stand still for an instant; Dilu neighs long and leaps through a flash of gold, and you cry, "Dilu! Dilu! Will you doom me today?" — **it wards off one deadly blow, restores half your troops and grants brief invulnerability**, helping you cheat death!

### 4. Famed Steeds of the Realm (Gallop Free, Rally the Cavalry)
In the chaos of the Three Kingdoms, every famed general needs a fine steed. When you pick up a famed steed on the battlefield, you can **swing into the saddle** and ride (a steed's bonuses apply only while mounted; if you are hit and thrown from the horse, you return to fighting on foot):
- **【Might of the Mounted — Lone Charge and Cavalry Rally】**:
  - If a **【Cavalry Camp】 is stationed** on the field: you whip your horse and command the cavalry! The Cavalry Camp's Attack Speed surges at once, and every so often several elite riders ride out from the camp and make a **rallying charge** toward your position, smashing the rebels along the way to pieces!
  - If there is **no 【Cavalry Camp】** on the field: you ride alone across the land! While mounted, your Move Speed and Attack greatly increase, and your steed is so magnificent that any rebel you run into while riding is hurt by the charging horse and flung aside!
- **Legendary Steeds of Every Hue**:
  - **【Red Hare】**: "Among men, Lü Bu; among horses, Red Hare." Its whole body is red as glowing coal, covering a thousand li by day and eight hundred by night, and it grants you a brief burst of extreme speed.
  - **【Blood-Sweat Steed】**: The heavenly horse of Dayuan in the Western Regions, which sweats blood as it runs. Its stamina is endless, helping you gallop at speed across the battlefield for a long time.
  - **【Flying Lightning】**: The divine steed most beloved of Cao Cao, King of Wei, snow-white with hooves like gold. Its explosive power is astonishing, turning into a flash of lightning in an instant to carry you out of the thick of a thousand armies in the blink of an eye!
  - **【Dapple Black】**: The beloved steed of Xiang Yu, the Hegemon-King of Western Chu, black as satin with snow-white hooves. Astride it, both your attack power and your marching speed are boosted.
  - **【Night-Shining Jade Lion】**: The dragon steed ridden by Zhao Zilong of Changshan, flawlessly snow-white and gleaming in the night. While you ride, the dragon horse's spirit guards you and reduces damage from enemy blades.
  - **【Wooden Ox and Gliding Horse】**: The ingenious grain-hauling contraption Zhuge Liang, Marquis Wu, devised at Mount Qi. It greatly widens the range in which you automatically gather the copper coins, ingots and gems on the battlefield.
  - **【Jueying】**: The divine steed that carried Cao Cao through the bloody fight at Wancheng, galloping on unfallen with several arrows in its body. Astride Jueying, you become **slightly faster in both attack and footwork**; but the moment you are hit, you are thrown from the horse and the bonus vanishes.
  - **【Eight Steeds】**: The eight divine horses King Mu of Zhou rode on his tours of the realm in high antiquity (Chiji, Daoli, Baiyi, Yulun, Shanzi, Quhuang, Hualiu and Lü'er). Each one found on the battlefield permanently strengthens your footwork and your blade, growing stronger the more you gather!

### 5. Secret Canons of the Strategists (War Manuals: Enlightening the Mind, Palm-Wind That Breaks Foes)
"Read ten thousand books; travel ten thousand li." War manuals of every age, found on the battlefield or bought at an ancient temple, each permanently raise your **"Intelligence"** (the higher it is, the faster your martial skills cool down and the wider and longer your Brocade Pouch stratagems reach — and with wisdom you also command the armies, raising the attack and defense of all your camps across the board!), and each volume carries its own special blessing:
- **【Spring and Autumn Annals】**: The classic compiled by the Sage Confucius, which Guan Yunchang read by candlelight through the night. Its subtle words and great principles deepen your wisdom and add a breath of righteous spirit, raising your **own basic attack power**.
- **【Art of War】**: The thirteen chapters of Sun Tzu, the Sage of War, ancestor of all military science. Steeped in the principle "swift as the wind, fierce as fire in conquest," it greatly raises the **Attack of your camps**.
- **【Mengde's New Book】**: The war manual Cao Cao wrote from a lifetime in the saddle, stressing swiftness in the use of troops and victory by surprise. It greatly raises the **Attack Speed of your camps**.
- **【Sun Bin's Art of War】**: Written by Sun Bin of the Warring States, skilled in laying out fortified formations and resting while the enemy tires. It lifts the **defensive resilience of your camps** to a new level.
- **【Jiang Taigong's Strategies】**: The true transmission of the *Six Secret Teachings* of Jiang Taigong of the great Zhou. It instructs you in the breathing and force-channeling arts, making **your own sword and spear strokes** faster still.
- **【Marquis Wu's Strategies】**: Zhuge Liang's lifelong masterwork on governing the army, all-embracing in scope. It raises the **Attack and Attack Speed of both you and the three camps** together.
- **【Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms】**: The supreme martial codex of another realm, drifted into the chaos of the Three Kingdoms — one in ten thousand! Once you have mastered this scroll, not only does your basic attack power soar, but each time you swing a blade or thrust a spear, the true qi surging within you erupts toward **directly behind you** as a white-gray **palm wave** that grows from small to mountain-moving, hurling any rebels sneaking up from behind a hundred paces away. A truly peerless divine art that guards front and back alike!

## V. The Yellow Turban Chieftains and the Three Difficulties

### 1. General Rules for Chieftains
- **Warding Aura**: Every Yellow Turban chieftain is shielded by sorcery. Martial skills, camps and treasures can strip only a limited amount of health per second, and any excess is **"deflected by the ward"** in a flash of gold. Only your own **basic attacks**, blade and spear in hand, can break through at the root, stroke by stroke.
- **Contact Means Damage**: Collide head-on with a chieftain and you take his basic attack full force, so do not be greedy about closing in.
- **After the Chieftain Falls**: When a chieftain is put to the sword and "Chieftain Vanquished" is declared, the Grand Chest he leaves behind drops not far from you and can be opened only after the triumph sequence ends; an Ancient Temple then appears.

### 2. Zhang Bao, General of Earth (Wine, Lust, Wealth and Rage)
- **【Dying for Gold】**: Zhang Bao bellows "Men die for wealth!" and showers coins from his gourd across the sky (anyone hit loses troops). He then sucks every Yellow Turban grunt on the field into the gourd and spews them out again as scattering **ghost Skeleton Soldiers**, and touching one costs you troops.
- **【Fox Spirit Charm】**: After wealth comes beauty — from the gourd fly a dancer and a musician, who coil around you, immune to blade and spear. While entangled you move sluggishly, your martial skill cooldowns run at half speed, and morale drains away bit by bit. A flung dancing sleeve cuts your offense sharply, and a pipa sound wave pins you in place and resets all your skill cooldowns. **The only way to break it: chase down Zhang Bao and slash him with several basic attacks** (a "Spell Break" count appears above his head), and the two beauties vanish like smoke on the spot.
- Zhang Bao dislikes close combat and always backs away to maneuver after a move, so you must pursue him relentlessly.

### 3. Zhang Jiao, General of Heaven (Master of the Way of Great Peace)
- **【Talisman Ward】**: Two rings of talismans circle Zhang Jiao, greatly weakening your basic attacks. Each hit knocks one talisman off, but after a moment they slowly grow back — **keep slashing while you have the momentum** to break his ward.
- **The "Bound"Seal on the Talisman**: Anyone struck by Zhang Jiao's talisman is bound in place, unable to walk or use basic attacks, and all martial skill cooldowns start over.
- **【Heavenly Thunder Array】**: Zhang Jiao chants and works his rite, and heaven and earth change color — tornadoes bounce across the field, lightning strikes spread over the ground like ripples, and three Zhang Jiaos fire homing talismans at once. As the incantation nears its end, black clouds gather and thunder rumbles, and Zhang Jiao bellows **"Fall!"** — and heavenly thunder splits down at the word! When you hear the incantation nearing its end, run for your life.

### 5. The Three Difficulties
- **Easy**: The rebels are weaker, suited to lords new to the battlefield or who wish to admire the martial skills.
- **Normal**: The standard campaign.
- **Hell**: Rebel attack and defense surge and the chieftains grow fiercer. After taking a hit you get one second of breathing-room invulnerability, and gold, experience and treasure drops are also richer — but **a hero can learn at most three martial skills**, as one of the four skill slots must be reserved for a camp.
