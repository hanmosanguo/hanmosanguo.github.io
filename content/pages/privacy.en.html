The game: Ten Thousand Foes

This game does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • The game contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Store platforms (such as Apple, Google and Valve) handle data related to your purchase or download under their own privacy policies; that data does not pass through us.

This website

  • This website uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics. Fonts and images are served from this site, not loaded from third parties.
  • Your chosen display language is saved only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never sent to us.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log connection information (such as IP addresses) for security and operations — see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Clicking a Discord link takes you to Discord's website, which is covered by Discord's own privacy policy.

Email you send us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you. We never use them for anything else or share them with third parties.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal data at all, we collect none from children either.
